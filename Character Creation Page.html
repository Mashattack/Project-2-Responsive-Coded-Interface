Race

  • Weak and feeble creatures with no unique abilities, humans lives are often short and filled with hardship.

  • Reanimated corpses, the undead are often driven by a desire for revenge or a need to fulfill a dark purpose.

  • Half-vampire, dhampirs are known for their enhanced physical abilities and a connection to both the living and the undead.

Class

  • Strong and dependable, knights excel at defense, combat, and leadership.

  • Necromancers command the forces of death and decay, raising the dead and manipulating dark magic.

  • Quick and clever, assassins are masters of stealth, trickery, and precision.

Background

  • Disgraced aristocrat whose family was slaughtered for political gain.

  • Scholars are driven by knowledge, curiosity, and a love of learning.

  • Outlanders grew up in the wild, making them resourceful, tough, and deeply connected to nature.

Appearance

  • The eyes of someone who has seen much and carries the weight of their experiences.

  • Scarred from past battles or trials, the mark of a survivor.

  • The mark of a person who has been marked by their past, whether through punishment, initiation, or a significant life event.
