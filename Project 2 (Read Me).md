I made it for gamers that enjoy more dark fantasy and spooky games, its made to be simple to use and navigate.

What should the user or player be able to do or understand?

They should be able to navigate each section clearly, read about teh different selctions and made one to build their character.
## Inspiration Interfaces
List the inspiration interfaces you used and briefly explain what you learned from them.

I maily took inspiration from the Lord of the Rings Online Character Creation screen and how they set up everything and nagvigated it. I learned that its important to add descriptions so that players can get a better idea what they are selelcting.
## Design Choices
- Layout: The layout is simple with the title on top, race and background to the right, and class and apperance to the left.
- Color: I mainlt went with dark colors like black, dark grey and reds to fit with the gothic esthetic I was going for.
- Typography: I used more victorian text like UnifrakturCook to add to the dark fantasy feel.
- Visual hierarchy: I had it to where the main choices you would make for your character would be in the middle since thats where your eye would navigate to.
- Feedback states: When you highlight over an interactable section it lights up a slight bit, you can also open up lists to read up on things and type your characters name in the bottom.
- Responsive design: The sections shrink when you shrink the page in oder to better fit everything in it.
